MCT Analytical Studio

A private business automation system used to process advertising phone calls, generate transcripts and structured analysis, and export approved text-based analysis files to Google Drive.

What the application does

MCT Analytical Studio receives call packages from the company's telephony and CRM workflow, performs transcription and business analysis on MCT-controlled infrastructure, and uses Google Drive as a destination for selected analysis artifacts.

Google Drive access

The Google OAuth connection is used by the application's server-side rclone transport. The workflow is configured to work with the designated MCT advertising-call analysis folder and the files required for that export workflow.

The application does not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, resale, credit decisions, or training general-purpose AI models.
